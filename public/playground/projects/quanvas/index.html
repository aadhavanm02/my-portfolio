Live app · Gemini

Use it like you would on your phone

Look-ups are answered live by AI, so they can take a few seconds and can be wrong. Your canvases stay in this browser only.

  • Look anything upType an item or a question, like “kasuri methi” or “how long to soak chana?”, then tap the Look up pill.
  • Get any recipeType “how to make paneer butter masala”. Open the card to cook step by step or buy the ingredients.
  • Ask about a photo or linkUse the + button to add a photo of an ingredient, a text file or a recipe link, then tap What is this? or Explain.
