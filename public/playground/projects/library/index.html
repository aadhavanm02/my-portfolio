Walk in and borrow a book

A 3D library you walk through. Books with a ribbon have their real public-domain opening; the rest show sample pages. Your card and books stay in this browser.

  • Make your library cardPick a name and a look, then tap Walk in.
  • Walk the racksArrow keys or WASD on desktop, the joystick on a phone. Walk up to a rack to pick up a book.
  • Read or borrowRead at the table for an hour, or sign the register at the desk and take the book home.
  • Turn pagesTwo pages on desktop, one on a phone. Swipe, tap or use the arrow keys, and bookmark with the ribbon.
