Live app · Gemini

Use it like a job seeker would

Resume reading, tailoring, refining and referral notes run live on AI. Jobs and referrers are example companies. Everything stays in this browser.

  • Start with your resumeUpload a PDF or paste the text, or pick the sample profile to jump in.
  • Tailor it to a jobOpen a job, tap Tailor resume, then accept or reject each suggestion.
  • Refine with chatAsk for changes in plain words. It won't add anything your resume doesn't support.
  • Ask for a referralOpen Referrals and send a note to someone with an open slot.
